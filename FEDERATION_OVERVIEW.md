## What This Is

The arifOS Federation is a governed intelligence substrate — seven specialized repositories working as one system under 13 constitutional floors (F1–F13). It separates authority from capability: the kernel judges, the cockpit routes, the forge executes, and domain organs provide evidence.

**This is not a chatbot framework. It is a constitutional governance layer between agents and irreversible action.**

```

  ↓
arifOS (Kernel — judges: SEAL / HOLD / SABAR / VOID)
  ↓
Domain Organs (GEOX / WEALTH / WELL — compute evidence only)
  ↓
A-FORGE (Executor — builds, deploys after SEAL)
  ↓
VAULT999 (Immutable ledger — append-only, hash-chained)
```

**Iron rules:**
1. No organ self-authorizes
2. No organ skips the chain
3. arifOS judges → A-FORGE executes → VAULT999 records
4. Domain organs compute evidence, never adjudicate

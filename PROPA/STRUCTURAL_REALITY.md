## 2. Mutual Separation Scheme (MSS) — Official Pathway

**Source:** myPETRONAS Exit Management page (Exit-Management.aspx)

**Eligibility criteria (Company may, at its discretion, offer MSS to employees who meet):**

| Clause | Criterion |
|--------|-----------|
| I | Employee whose work performance deteriorates over time due to poor health, psychologically unstable illness, long-term medical treatment or prolonged illness leave, but does not fall under Medical Board Out condition |
| II | Employee who has reached maximum potential and/or maximum salary |
| III | Employee whose functional skill is not relevant to present business requirements |
| IV | Employee with more than 20 years of service, age 50 and above at planned attrition date, with undifferentiated performance, for the purpose of rejuvenation of the organisation and/or unblocking progression of others |
| V | Any other situation(s) as deemed appropriate by the Company |

**Initiation:** "The conversation on Mutual Separation Scheme may be initiated by either the Company or the Employee."

**Process (from LRO FAQ 28 April 2025):**

- Q 2.3 — Can employees volunteer for VSS/MSS? "Under the current policy, employees can initiate interest for the MSS and discuss with respective superior if they meet the eligible criteria. The application will be subject to necessary process and approval."
- Q 2.4 — Can an employee eligible for MSS opt for a mapped position instead? "Employees who are not placed will be offered MSS as an option. For those who choose not to accept, we will try to place them based on best effort. In cases where that does not materialise, they will unfortunately have to transition out of the organisation through the retrenchment process. Fair compensation and support will be provided to help navigate their next steps."
- Q 2.1 — Bonded scholars: "Should PETRONAS scholars be offered an MSS, the company remains committed to supporting the impacted employees throughout the transition."

**Voluntary vs Involuntary:**
- Voluntary (MSS) — employee-initiated conversation possible under Clauses I–V above; Company retains discretion to accept or decline.
- Involuntary (retrenchment) — triggered when placement fails and MSS is declined; framed as "transition out through the retrenchment process" with "fair compensation and support."
